What We Do

Permitting, mapping, and title work — handled by people who've done it for decades.

01

Seismic Permitting & Right-of-Way Acquisition

Over 40 years permitting projects in 23 states, using a proven team approach on every job — with an experienced manager overseeing each project directly and communicating with you throughout.

  • A large team of mineral agents, surface agents, GIS mappers/coordinators, and support staff
  • Comprehensive reporting and direct manager oversight on every project
  • Landowner payment services — check generation and tracking, delivery to landowners, 1099 generation, and required IRS filings
  • Direct coordination with municipal, county, state, and federal agencies
02

GIS Services

Experienced GIS mappers and coordinators, working with current software, build a comprehensive database and map for every project — so nothing about the land is a surprise once work begins.

  • Surface, mineral, and infrastructure mapping in one coordinated dataset
  • Topographic detail alongside ownership records for every tract
  • Project maps built and maintained as work progresses, not just at kickoff
03

Title Curative

On any exploration or production project, confirming the correct chain of mineral ownership on every tract is one of the most critical — and most easily overlooked — tasks. Our title agents, working with us around the country, make sure a title issue never disrupts your project.

  • Mineral ownership determination for permitting purposes
  • Full title search back to sovereignty when a project requires it
  • Land ownership, mineral rights, and surface title opinions
