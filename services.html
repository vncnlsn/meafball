What We Do

Permitting, mapping, and title work — handled by people who've done it for decades.

Across a Project

We're involved before the first survey stake goes in the ground, and we stay involved after.

APS evaluates the land, secures the access, and keeps the project's information organized — then stays engaged once crews are in the field, so the work matches the plan, the permits, and the agreements behind it.

  1. 01 Project Planning & Evaluation Geography, topography, and regulatory requirements assessed up front.
  2. 02 Land & Ownership Research Mineral and surface ownership identified, tract by tract.
  3. 03 Permitting & Access Agreements and permits secured with landowners and agencies.
  4. 04 Field Preparation Access, records, and reporting in place before crews mobilize.
  5. 05 Field Operations Work tracked against the plan, safety requirements, and agreements.
  6. 06 Project Closeout Records, payments, and reporting reconciled and delivered.

GIS runs underneath all six stages — ownership, topographic, and infrastructure data stay current from first evaluation through closeout, not just at kickoff.

02 — Title & Ownership

Title Curative

On any exploration or production project, confirming the correct chain of mineral ownership on every tract is one of the most critical — and most easily overlooked — tasks. Our title agents, working with us around the country, make sure a title issue never disrupts your project.

Primary Service

Title Curative

Where a title issue could interfere with permitting or hold up a project, we research it and work it through — so it's resolved before it becomes a delay.

Primary Service

Ownership Research

We research mineral and surface ownership on every tract, including the chain-of-title work needed to determine the ownership a permit requires — full title search back to sovereignty when a project calls for it.

Primary Service

Title Opinions

Land ownership, mineral rights, and surface title opinions, prepared by experienced title agents working with us around the country.

Also Included
