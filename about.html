About APS Land

Who We Are

Four decades of permitting and land access work, built one landowner conversation at a time.

Our Story

Built on Conversations, Not Just Contracts

APS Land was founded in 1980 to help energy and infrastructure companies do something that sounds simple and rarely is: get permission to build, cross, or work on land that belongs to someone else. That work starts long before any equipment arrives. Our land agents are usually the first people a landowner meets — they explain what's being proposed, walk the property in person, and negotiate access and compensation before a single permit is ever filed.

From there, our permit specialists take over the regulatory side, coordinating directly with municipal, county, state, and federal agencies so the paperwork never falls on the landowner. Our GIS team maps routes and boundaries with enough precision to keep disruption to a minimum, and our title agents confirm chain of ownership so a project never stalls over a land record nobody checked.

It's a way of working we've carried through every project since 1980: show up in person, explain the plan plainly, and treat the landowner as a partner in the project rather than an obstacle to it.

Our History

Four Decades in the Field

1980

APS Land opens its doors, taking on its first permitting and land access work in Texas.

1990s

Our team of mineral and surface agents grows, and work expands into neighboring states.

2000s

GIS mapping joins the process, sharpening how routes are planned and disruption is minimized.

2010s

Title curative services are added, so ownership questions get settled before crews arrive.

Today

Work carried out in 23 states, with the same manager-led, in-person approach we started with.
