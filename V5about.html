Who We Are

Our Story

Built on Conversations, Not Just Contracts

APS Land was founded in 1980 to help energy and infrastructure companies do something that sounds simple and rarely is: get permission to build, cross, or work on land that belongs to someone else. That work starts long before any equipment arrives. Our land agents are usually the first people a landowner meets — they explain what's being proposed, walk the property in person, and negotiate access and compensation before a single permit is ever filed.

From there, our permit specialists take over the regulatory side, coordinating directly with state and federal agencies so the paperwork never falls on the landowner. Our GIS and land management team maps routes and boundaries with enough precision to keep disruption to a minimum, and our field representatives stay on site through construction — so there's always one familiar person to call, rather than a rotating cast of contractors.

It's a way of working we've carried through every project since 1980: show up in person, explain the plan plainly, and treat the landowner as a partner in the project rather than an obstacle to it.

Our History

Four Decades in the Field

1980

APS Land opens its doors, taking on its first permitting and land access work in Texas.

1990s

Land agents begin building relationships with landowners across neighboring states.

2000s

Leasing and title research join permitting, so ownership questions get settled before crews arrive.

2010s

GIS and land management join the team, sharpening how routes are mapped and disruption is minimized.

Today

Field representatives stay on site nationwide, giving landowners one steady point of contact from start to finish.
